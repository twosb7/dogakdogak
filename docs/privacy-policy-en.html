Dogakdogak Keyboard Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 7, 2026 | Last Updated: March 7, 2026

1. Overview

"Dogakdogak Keyboard" ("the App") is an Android keyboard app. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed on-device, what data is synced to our servers, and how users can request deletion.

Important: the App does not collect, store, or transmit the text content that users type, including sentences, words, passwords, or clipboard content, to Dogakdogak servers.

2. Data We Collect or Sync

2-1. Account and Profile Data

DataWhenPurposeRetention
Email addressWhen signing in with Google or KakaoAccount identification and sign-inUntil account deletion
NicknameWhen entered by the userProfile and leaderboard displayUntil account deletion
Avatar URL and uploaded avatar imageWhen set by the userProfile and leaderboard displayUntil account deletion

2-2. Ranking and Usage Statistics

DataDefault / OptionalPurposeRetention

2-3. Purchase Data

DataPurposeRetention

3. Data We Do Not Send to Dogakdogak Servers

  • Typed text, passwords, sentences, or clipboard content
  • Precise location data
  • Advertising ID, IMEI, or similar device identifiers
  • Per-app usage statistics without explicit consent
  • Installed app names for suggestions
  • Raw contacts data
  • Raw microphone audio

4. Permissions and On-Device Processing

4-1. Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used only when the user activates voice input, and the app shows an in-app explanation before requesting permission. Audio is not uploaded to Dogakdogak servers. Voice recognition may be handled by the operating system or the voice service selected by the user.

4-2. Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)

Used only if the user enables contacts-based suggestions, confirms the in-app explanation, and grants permission. Contacts are used on-device to improve suggestions and corrections and are not uploaded to Dogakdogak servers.

4-3. Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used for the combo overlay feature and only when enabled by the user.

4-4. Internet (INTERNET)

Used for sign-in, ranking sync, avatar upload, purchase verification, and account deletion.

5. Why We Use Data

  • Authentication and account management
  • Global and per-app leaderboards
  • Profile display
  • Purchase verification and restoration
  • Service stability and support

6. Service Providers

We do not sell personal information for advertising. We rely on the following processors to operate the service:

  • Supabase: account, profile, leaderboard, and deletion processing
  • Google Play: in-app purchases and purchase verification
  • Google / Kakao: social authentication

7. Security

  • Server communications use HTTPS/TLS.
  • Account deletion is processed server-side to remove profile, leaderboard data, per-app stats, purchase sync records, and avatar files together.
  • Local settings and tokens are stored on the device.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their account and related data in either of these ways:

  • In-app: Settings screen → Delete account
  • Web instructions: https://twosb7.github.io/dogakdogak/delete-account.html

Deletion covers profile data, ranking totals, per-app ranking stats, purchase sync records, and avatar images associated with the account.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. Material changes will be announced in the app or on the public pages.
