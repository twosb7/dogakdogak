Turn every keystroke into a satisfying experience.

Dogakdogak Keyboard adds ASMR sound effects, a real-time combo counter,
and global rankings to your everyday typing — making it genuinely fun
to type.

★ ASMR Sound Effects
Choose from mechanical keyboard clicks, bubble pops, chill tones, and
more. Each keypress sounds exactly how it feels.

★ Combo System
Keep typing to build up combos. A floating overlay shows your streak
in real time — the longer the chain, the bigger the reward.

★ Global Rankings
Compete with typists worldwide. See where you rank daily, weekly,
monthly, or all-time.

★ Social Login
Sign in with Google or Kakao to sync your stats and join the rankings.

★ Keyboard Customization
Themes, colors, layouts, one-handed mode, split keyboard, number row —
fully customizable to your preference.

★ Multilingual
Korean, English, and many more language layouts supported.

★ Autocorrect &amp; Spell Check
Suggestions, corrections, emoji search, and clipboard history included.

PRIVACY
- Your typed text is never collected or transmitted.
- Only your keystroke count is synced (for rankings).
- Profile (nickname, avatar) is optional.

Built on HeliBoard, an open-source keyboard based on AOSP / OpenBoard
(GPL v3.0). Source code available on GitHub.
