Turn everyday typing into something satisfying.

Dogakdogak Keyboard combines ASMR typing sounds, a combo overlay,
and rankings to make your keyboard feel more alive.

★ ASMR Sound Effects
Choose from mechanical clicks, bubble sounds, chill tones, and more.

★ Combo Overlay
Build combos while typing and see your streak in real time.

★ Global Rankings
After sign-in, you can sync score and touch totals to join daily,
weekly, monthly, and all-time leaderboards.

★ Per-App Rankings
Per-app typing/touch counts are synced only after explicit consent,
and only for supported apps. Typed text content is not included.

★ Social Login
Sign in with Google or Kakao to sync your records.

★ Keyboard Features
Korean and English input, autocorrect, emoji search, clipboard history,
one-handed mode, and customization options are included.

PRIVACY
- Typed text content is never collected or transmitted.
- Nickname and avatar are optional.
- Installed app names are not read or uploaded for suggestions.
- Microphone permission may be used for voice input only after an in-app explanation.
- Contacts permission may be used for contact-based suggestions only after an in-app explanation.
- Account deletion is available both in-app and through the public deletion page.

Built on HeliBoard, an open-source keyboard based on AOSP / OpenBoard (GPL v3.0).
